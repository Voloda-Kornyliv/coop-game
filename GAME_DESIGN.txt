=====================================================================
  BLAST PARTY
  Game design document, v0.4, 2026-10-14
=====================================================================

  A top-down arena shooter for 2-8 friends in the same room. Everyone
  plays in the browser on their own phone or laptop. Play
  every-player-for-themselves or Team vs Team.

  Rounds are short and chaotic, there are lots of silly abilities, and
  your character grows stronger over the match through XP, levels and
  stacking upgrades. The aim is to make people laugh, shout and
  immediately want "one more round".

  CHANGES IN v0.4
    - PURE AUTO-RELOAD: Removed manual reload (R key) so phones and
      laptops are 100% equal. Guns auto-reload when empty, or trickle-
      reload one bullet at a time when you stop shooting for 0.8s.
      Dashing while empty reloads 2 bullets instantly (section 6).
    - SUDDEN DEATH EVENTS: When the zone closes completely, a chaotic
      modifier triggers (Mega Knockback, Banana Rain, etc.) so rounds
      end in laughter instead of slow zone-healing stalemates (section 9).
    - MATCH POINT "BOSS MODE": A player one win away from taking the
      match gets a glowing aura and boss music, uniting the room
      against them (section 12).
    - GHOST POSSESSION: Ghosts can now hop inside crates and barrels
      to wiggle them around and scare living players (section 11).
    - UPGRADE SYNERGIES: Combining specific upgrades unlocks secret
      bonus effects (section 8B).
    - BALANCE & ANTI-CAMPING: Bushes burn away when hit by explosions;
      the Crown holder pulses on the minimap instead of being permanently
      tracked; odd-numbered teams get a "Short-Handed" buff; resolved
      all open questions from v0.3 (sections 9, 12, 17).

  CHANGES IN v0.3
    - Smoother play on bad internet: game data uses a separate channel
      that never waits for lost packets, your own movement is predicted
      on your device, and a ping indicator is shown (section 15).
    - The gun has a magazine and reloads; fewer bullets per second
      (section 6). Two new upgrades: EXTENDED MAG and FAST HANDS.
    - Players move 10% slower.
    - More walls on every map (section 10).
    - Picking up an item shows a small notification at the bottom
      instead of a big announcement (section 8C).

  CHANGES IN v0.2
    - Bigger maps, following camera, minimap, XP and levels, turrets,
      barrels, XP Vault, bushes, Team vs Team mode, stronger catch-up
      rules, reconnect rules, and implementation status.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESIGN PILLARS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. FUNNY BEFORE FAIR
     Abilities should make the room laugh: slipping on a banana,
     getting turned into a sheep, swapping places at the worst moment.
     When a balance choice comes up, pick the funnier option, as long
     as it doesn't feel cheap.

  2. PICK UP AND PLAY IN 10 SECONDS
     Open the link, type your name, enter the code, play. No accounts,
     installs or tutorials. The controls explain themselves.

  3. PHONE IS A FIRST-CLASS CONTROLLER
     Every feature must work well with two thumbs on a phone. Laptops
     are supported, but nothing may need a mouse or keyboard, and
     laptop players must never have extra buttons that phones don't.

  4. SHORT ROUNDS, NOBODY WAITS LONG
     Rounds last about 60-120 seconds. Eliminated players keep playing
     as ghosts, so nobody just sits and watches.

  5. EVERYONE SEES THE SAME AMOUNT OF THE WORLD
     The camera follows you around a map that is bigger than your
     screen. Every device sees the same area of the world, whether it's
     a phone or a laptop, so a bigger screen is never an advantage.
     A minimap makes sure you always know where to go.

  6. NOBODY IS EVER OUT OF IT
     Players get stronger during a match, but the leader is slowed down
     and players who are behind are boosted. Winning many rounds in a
     row should get harder and harder. Coming back from behind should
     always feel possible.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GAME MODES
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  The host picks the mode in the lobby.

  FREE-FOR-ALL (default, 2-8 players)
    Every player for themselves. The last player alive wins the round.

  TEAM VS TEAM (4-8 players)
    - Two teams: RED and BLUE. The lobby fills them alternately. A
      player can tap the team badge to switch sides, and the host has a
      "Shuffle teams" button.
    - At START the host's device rebalances the teams so their sizes
      differ by at most 1.
    - ODD-PLAYER BALANCE ("Underdog Squad"): If teams are uneven (e.g.,
      2 vs 3), the smaller team gets +25% max HP, +15% reload speed,
      and their Revive timer is cut in half (1.5s instead of 3s).
    - The round goes to the last team with a living player. A team
      wins the match after the chosen number of round wins (3 / 5 / 7).
    - No friendly fire: damage never hurts your teammates. Chaos
      effects that don't do damage still affect everyone, including
      teammates: banana slips, Black Hole pull, Bounce Pad, Teleport
      All. Friends will betray each other by accident, which is the
      fun of it.
    - Teammates are always visible: on the minimap, with a team-colour
      ring around them, and with arrows at the screen edge.
    - Each player keeps their own colour. A team-coloured ring and
      team-coloured name show which side they're on.
    - REVIVE: a living player who stays within 60 units of a
      teammate's ghost for 3 seconds brings them back with 40% HP. Each
      player can be revived once per round. The ghost can float over to
      a teammate to make this easier.
    - Teams spawn on opposite sides of the map.
    - All catch-up rules (section 12) apply per team instead of per
      player.
    - There's no crown in team mode. The team that is ahead gets the
      leader rules instead (section 12).


---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CORE LOOP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  LOBBY -> DRAFT -> ROUND -> RESULTS -> DRAFT -> ROUND -> ... -> WINNER

  - The host creates a game, and friends join with the code or the
    invite link.
  - In the lobby the host picks the mode (Free-for-all / Teams), the
    match length (first to 3 / 5 / 7 round wins) and the map (Random
    or a specific one). Every player picks a hat.
  - First draft: everyone picks their ACTIVE ability.
  - ROUND: a 3-2-1 countdown, then everyone spawns spread around the
    map. Explore, smash things for XP, find each other and fight. After
    40 seconds the zone starts closing, so rounds can't drag on.
  - The last player (or team) alive wins the round and gets +1 point.
  - DRAFT between rounds: everyone EXCEPT the round winner picks an
    upgrade (section 8). In team mode, the whole winning team skips
    it.
  - Upgrades, XP and levels all carry over for the whole match, so by
    the last rounds everyone has a lot of HP and damage.
  - The first to reach the target number of round wins takes the
    match. Then come the results and awards (section 13), with "Play
    again" or "Back to lobby".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CAMERA, MAP SIZE AND MINIMAP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Camera
    - Top-down, follows your own player smoothly.
    - FAIR VIEW: every device sees the same 720 units of world height.
      The width follows the screen shape but is capped at 1440 units,
      so a wider screen gets a little more side view but never more
      than that. A phone shows the same area as a laptop, just
      smaller.
    - The camera looks slightly ahead toward where you're aiming (up
      to 15% of the view), so you can see what you're shooting at.
    - At the map edge the camera stops moving instead of showing
      empty space.
    - Ghosts get a wider view (1.5x) and can fly anywhere, so they're
      good spectators.
    - Players are 44 units across, so on a phone they come out about
      25 px tall, much bigger than in v0.1 where the whole map fit on
      screen.

  Map size
    - Maps are about 2800 x 1600 to 3200 x 1800 units, roughly 4 times
      the v0.1 arena.
    - With 2-3 players the zone starts closing earlier (at 25 seconds
      instead of 40), so small games don't turn into hide-and-seek.

  Minimap (top-right corner, about 120 x 68 px on a phone)
    - Always shows: walls, the zone circle and where it will end up,
      you, your teammates, turrets that are still alive, the XP Vault,
      and the Golden Comeback Crate (if spawned).
    - Enemies are NOT shown by default. An enemy appears as a short
      blip for 1 second whenever they shoot.
    - CROWN RADAR: The crown holder (the leader in free-for-all)
      pulses on everyone's minimap for 1.5 seconds every 4 seconds.
      Leading makes you hunted, but pulsing lets the leader still pull
      off quick jukes and bush ambushes between pings (section 12).

  Off-screen indicators
    - Small arrows at the screen edge point to teammates, the crown
      holder (during radar pulses), and any enemy who hit you in the
      last 2 seconds. That way you know where a shot came from.

  Phones play in LANDSCAPE. In portrait, the game shows a "rotate your
  phone" screen. Your own character has a white outline and a small
  arrow above it.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CONTROLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  PHONE (landscape, touch)
    Left thumb:   virtual joystick to MOVE. It appears wherever you
                  first touch on the left half of the screen.
    Right thumb:  virtual joystick to AIM. Holding it pushed out fires
                  automatically. When you're not aiming, your aim
                  follows the direction you're moving.
    Buttons on the right edge:
      [ABILITY]   your active ability. Tap to cast it in your aim
                  direction, or drag from the button to aim precisely.
      [DASH]      a short burst of speed with brief invulnerability.
      [PICKUP]    use your single-use item (greyed out when you have
                  none).
    Emote buttons sit at the bottom centre.
    Extras: the phone vibrates when you get hit (Android only), the
    game goes fullscreen (where the browser allows it) and the screen
    stays awake while you play.

  LAPTOP (keyboard + mouse)
    WASD / arrow keys   move
    Mouse               aim
    Left click (hold)   shoot
    Space               dash
    Q or right click    ability
    E                   use pickup
    1-4                 emotes
    (Note: No manual reload key. Reloading is 100% automatic across
    all devices for fairness and simplicity.)

  GAMEPAD (planned): twin sticks, triggers to shoot, face buttons for
  abilities, using the browser's Gamepad API.

  FAIRNESS BETWEEN PHONE AND MOUSE
    - Bullets are large, fairly slow and easy to see, so dodging
      matters more than pinpoint aim.
    - Touch aiming gets light aim assist: shots bend up to 12 degrees
      toward the nearest enemy.
    - Most abilities are area-based rather than precision-based.
    - Everyone sees the same amount of the world (section 4).


---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PLAYER BASICS & AUTO-RELOAD
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Health:       100 HP at level 1. Levels (section 7) and upgrades
                raise it.
  Basic gun:    about 3 shots per second, 20 damage per bullet at
                level 1.
  Auto-Reload:  8-shot magazine. There is NO reload button; reloading
                happens automatically in three intuitive ways:
                  1. EMPTY RELOAD: When you fire your last bullet, a
                     1.3-second full reload starts automatically. You
                     move 10% faster while completely empty so you can
                     duck behind cover!
                  2. TRICKLE RELOAD: If you stop shooting for 0.8
                     seconds with a part-empty magazine, bullets pop
                     back into your magazine one by one (every 0.2s)
                     with a satisfying rising "tick-tick-tick" sound.
                     Firing interrupts the trickle immediately—you
                     never get stuck in a reload animation when you
                     still have bullets left.
                  3. COMBAT ROLL: Dashing while reloading (or with a
                     part-empty mag) instantly loads 2 bullets into
                     your gun.
                Visuals: pips around your character's feet (or a curved
                bar under your HP) show your ammo, and a spinning ring
                shows an active empty-reload. The Minigun pickup
                doesn't use ammo.
  Movement:     responsive, with no inertia (except on ice). Speed is
                243 units per second.
  Dash:         about 200 units in 0.15 seconds, with a 2 second
                cooldown. You can't be hurt or affected while dashing.
  Knockback:    every hit pushes the target back a little. This adds
                comedy and lets you push people into hazards.
  Elimination:  the player bursts into confetti, a big "KO!" appears,
                and the kill feed shows a line like
                "Alex bananaed Bob".
  After death:  you become a GHOST (section 11).
  Above every player: hat (or crown), HP bar, level badge and status
  icons such as 🥔 Hot Potato, 🌀 Reverse and 📡 disconnected.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PROGRESSION: XP AND LEVELS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Smashing things gives XP. XP raises your level, and levels make you
  tougher and hit harder. XP and levels last for the WHOLE MATCH and
  reset when a new match starts.

  Where XP comes from
    Crate                    10 XP   dropped as orbs
    Explosive barrel         15 XP   dropped as orbs
    Turret                   40 XP   20 XP straight to killer, 20 XP as orbs
    XP Vault (1 per map)    120 XP   40 XP straight to breaker, 80 XP as orbs
    Knocking out a player    60 XP   given straight to whoever got the KO
    KO on the crown holder  +40 XP   bonus on top (section 12)
    First Blood             +25 XP   bonus for the first KO of a round

  XP ORBS & DECAY
    - Destroyed objects burst into glowing orbs. Giving part of the
      Turret/Vault XP straight to the breaker rewards the risk of
      shooting it, while the dropped orbs still cause a scramble.
    - Orbs fly to you when you're within 80 units (more with the
      MAGNET upgrade).
    - ORB DECAY: Orbs stay on the ground for 20 seconds. After 10
      seconds they start blinking and shrink to 50% value, vanishing
      at 20s. This stops players from camping near orb piles and pushes
      fast-paced collection.

  Level curve
    - Going from level N to N+1 costs 60 + 40 x N XP:
      L1 to L2: 100, L2 to L3: 140, L3 to L4: 180, ... L9 to L10: 420.
    - The maximum is level 10, about 2,340 XP in total.
    - A typical round gives about 150-200 XP, so a first-to-5 match
      ends with most players around level 7-10.

  What each level gives
    +12 max HP
    +6% damage (bullets AND abilities)
    Levelling up also heals 25 HP, triggers a small knockback wave
    around you, shows a "LEVEL UP!" burst, and plays a sound.

  At level 10 that adds up to +108 max HP and +54% damage, before
  upgrades.

  HUD: an XP bar under your HP bar, and your level number. Every
  player's level badge is shown next to their name.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. ABILITIES AND THE DRAFT
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Each player has three kinds of power:

    A) ONE ACTIVE ABILITY (the [ABILITY] button)
       Picked in the first draft. It has a cooldown, and a later draft
       may offer a different one.

    B) PASSIVE UPGRADES (stack for the whole match)
       Drafted between rounds. They are stored all match long and never
       lost.

    C) MAP PICKUPS (the [PICKUP] button)
       Single-use items from glowing "?" boxes. You can carry one at a
       time.

  Ability damage gets the level bonus (section 7).

  DRAFT RULES
    - Everyone picks at the same time, with a 15 second timer. When the
      timer runs out, a random card is picked.
    - The first draft (before round 1) offers ACTIVE abilities only.
      Every player gets 3 cards.
    - In later drafts each card has a 75% chance of being a passive
      upgrade and a 25% chance of being a different active ability.
    - How many cards you're offered depends on the score:
        Round winner (or the whole winning team)   0 cards
                                                   (skips the draft, but
                                                   gets a silly cosmetic
                                                   title for the round!)
        Match leader, if they didn't win the round 2 cards
        Everyone else                              3 cards
        Fewest round wins (if not tied with all)   4 cards (and 1 free
                                                   "Reroll" button)
    - While others pick, the winner (and anyone who has already
      picked) sees "Waiting for others…" with their current build.

  --- 8A. ACTIVE ABILITIES (cooldown 4-14 s) ---------------------------

  Movement
    BLINK         Short teleport in the aim direction.
    SWAP          Fire a slow orb. Whoever it hits swaps places with you.
    GRAPPLE       A hook that flies you to a wall, or yanks a player to
                  you (works on Explosive Barrels too!).
    BOUNCE PAD    Drop a pad. Anyone who steps on it (you included) is
                  launched across the map.

  Damage
    SHOTGUN       A wide burst of 7 pellets with big knockback.
    BOOMERANG     Flies out and comes back, hitting on both passes.
    HOMING ROCKET A slow rocket that chases the nearest enemy. It can be
                  shot down.
    CHAIN ZAP     Lightning that jumps between up to 4 nearby players.
    SNOWBALL      Grows bigger and stronger the further it rolls.
    BLACK HOLE    Pulls every other player and every bullet toward one
                  point for about 2 seconds.

  Defense
    BUBBLE        Blocks all damage and effects for 1.5 seconds.
    MIRROR        Reflects bullets back at the shooter for 1 second.
    DECOY         A fake you walks forward (and explodes when shot)
                  while you turn invisible for a moment.
    VANISH        Invisible for 3 seconds. Footprints still give you
                  away.

  Chaos (the funny ones)
    BANANA        Throw a banana peel. Anyone who steps on it slides
                  helplessly, spinning around and firing their gun in
                  random directions without using ammo.
    SHEEPIFY      Turns the target into a sheep for 2 seconds: no
                  shooting, only "baa", +20% movement speed to flee.
    BIG HEAD      Every enemy gets a huge head (a bigger hitbox) and
                  squeaky high-pitched sound effects for 4 seconds.
    SHRINK RAY    The target becomes tiny, fast and fragile for
                  4 seconds.
    REVERSE       The target's movement controls are reversed for
                  3 seconds.
    HOT POTATO    Sticks a bomb to someone. Touching another player
                  passes it on. It explodes after 5 seconds.
    DISCO BALL    Everyone near you is forced to dance for 1.5 seconds.
    FART CLOUD    A green cloud that hurts and pushes people away.

  --- 8B. PASSIVE UPGRADES (stack all match) ---------------------------

    BOUNCY BULLETS     Bullets bounce off walls (+1 bounce per stack).
    SPLIT SHOT         +1 bullet per shot, fired in a spread (-15% damage
                       per individual bullet so point-blank hits aren't
                       instant kills).
    BIG BULLETS        Bullets are 50% larger and deal +5 damage.
    FAST FEET          +15% movement speed.
    QUICK TRIGGER      +20% fire rate.
    VAMPIRE            Heal 4 HP whenever you hit an enemy player (2 HP
                       per pellet for Split Shot / Shotgun).
    THICK SKIN         +30 max HP, and you look chunkier.
    DASH MASTER        Half the dash cooldown. Dashing leaves a fire
                       trail and reloads +1 extra bullet.
    LAST LAUGH         Drop 3 live grenades when you die.
    PIERCING           Bullets pass through the first player/crate they hit.
    MAGNET             Pickups and XP orbs fly to you from further away,
                       and your bullets curve slightly toward enemies.
    COOLDOWN CLOCK     Ability cooldown -25%.
    TINY               25% smaller and harder to hit, -10 max HP.
    RICOCHET RAGE      Bullets speed up and deal +6 damage after each
                       wall hit.
    EXTENDED MAG       +4 shots in the magazine.
    FAST HANDS         Auto-reload and trickle-reload are 35% faster.
    PARTING GIFT (new) The last bullet in your magazine is 2x size,
                       explodes on impact, and deals +15 damage.

    UPGRADE SYNERGIES (Bonus fun when you collect specific pairs!)
      When a player drafts both upgrades in a pair, a golden "SYNERGY!"
      banner pops up and activates a free bonus effect:
      - PINBALL WIZARD (Bouncy Bullets + Ricochet Rage): Bullets auto-aim
        toward the nearest enemy after bouncing off a wall.
      - MOSQUITO (Tiny + Vampire): +10% movement speed and your character
        makes an annoying buzzing sound when near enemies.
      - SPRAY & PRAY (Split Shot + Extended Mag): Firing pushes you
        backwards slightly, letting you "jetpack" away while shooting.
      - JUGGERNAUT (Thick Skin + Big Bullets): Knockback against you is
        reduced by 50%, and you crush Wooden Crates just by walking
        through them.

  Each upgrade has a maximum number of stacks (2-3), so builds can't
  go completely out of control.

  --- 8C. MAP PICKUPS (single use) -------------------------------------

    MEDKIT        Restore 40 HP.
    MINIGUN       5 seconds of very fast fire with zero ammo cost.
    LANDMINE      Place a mine that turns invisible after 1.5 seconds.
    SPEED BOOTS   Much faster for 4 seconds, leaving lightning sparks.
    GIANT MODE    Grow huge for 4 seconds: push others around, crush
                  crates on contact, and take half damage.
    CONFUSION     Every enemy's screen flips upside down for 3 seconds.
    TELEPORT ALL  Every living player moves to a random safe spot.
    MYSTERY       Casts 2 random active abilities at the same time.

  You can't see what's inside a box until you grab it. Boxes spawn over
  time and sometimes drop from crates.

  When you grab or use an item, a small notification appears at the
  bottom of the screen (for example "🦖 Giant Mode: press E / tap 🎒").
  It never covers the middle of the screen.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ARENA OBJECTS & SUDDEN DEATH
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Bigger maps are filled with things to smash, avoid and fight over.

  CRATES
    Break after a few hits. They drop 10 XP of orbs and sometimes a
    pickup box. They block bullets and movement until broken. There
    are 25-40 per map.

  EXPLOSIVE BARRELS
    Red barrels with 40 HP. When destroyed they explode (radius 140,
    40 damage), can set off other barrels nearby, and drop 15 XP.
    Shooting a barrel takes 2 hits, OR 1 hit makes it hiss and burn
    for 1.5s before exploding—giving players a hilarious panic moment
    to dash away or grapple-throw it! There are 6-10 per map.

  TURRETS (small and destructible)
    - Stationary guns, 4-8 per map. They shoot at the nearest living
      player within 450 units that they can see. They ignore ghosts.
    - They are fair: the barrel glows for 0.4 seconds before each shot,
      and their bullets are slow and dodgeable.
    - Turret HP:      120 + 30 per round already played.
      Shot damage:    12 + 3 per round already played.
      They fire one shot every 1.2 seconds.
    - Destroyed turrets give 20 XP directly to the destroyer, drop 20 XP
      of orbs, and have a 30% chance to drop a pickup box. They don't
      come back until the next round.
    - Turrets are neutral and shoot everyone. A turret KO is credited to
      whoever hit the victim in the last 4 seconds; otherwise the
      victim "got turreted".

  XP VAULT (one per map)
    A big armoured chest near the middle of the map, with 400 HP and
    usually guarded by 2 turrets. It gives 40 XP to whoever lands the
    final blow and drops 80 XP of orbs. It gives every round a
    contested centre to fight over.

  BUSHES (tall grass — now flammable!)
    - Players standing in a bush are hidden from enemies unless the
      enemy is within 120 units.
    - Shooting or getting hit reveals you for 1 second.
    - ANTI-CAMPING: Explosions (Barrels, Rockets, Parting Gift, Dash
      Master fire trail) burn bushes down for the rest of the round!
      If someone is hiding, blow up their cover.

  HAZARDS (depend on the map)
    Lava pools, ice, conveyor belts, bumpers and darkness (section 10).
    Lava and the zone do damage as a % of max HP, so they stay
    dangerous in the last rounds when everyone has lots of HP.

  SHRINKING ZONE & SUDDEN DEATH MAYHEM
    - Starts closing after 40 seconds (25 seconds with 2-3 players) and
      shrinks over 45 seconds to a small final circle (radius 220).
    - Where the final circle ends up is random, but it is shown on the
      minimap from the start of the round.
    - Outside the zone you lose 8% of your max HP per second.
    - SUDDEN DEATH MAYHEM: Once the zone finishes shrinking to the final
      circle, if more than 1 player/team is still alive after 5 seconds,
      "⚡ SUDDEN DEATH! ⚡" flashes and one random mayhem event starts:
        * BANANA STORM: Bananas rain from the sky every second.
        * ZERO GRAVITY: Knockback from all bullets and hits is 400%.
        * METEOR SHOWER: Explosive barrels parachute into the circle.
        * HYPER SPEED: Everyone gets +50% movement speed and infinite ammo.
      After 15 seconds of Sudden Death, the circle shrinks to nothing.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
10. MAPS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Each round uses a random map, or the one the host locked in the
  lobby. Every map is mirrored so all spawn points are equally fair. In
  Team mode the two teams spawn on opposite halves.

  Every map has plenty of walls: cover to hide behind, corridors and
  corners to fight around. Open areas without any walls are kept small,
  so you can always find cover while your gun auto-reloads.

    BACKYARD      2800 x 1600   The starter map. Hedges (bushes), garden
                                sheds (walls), lots of crates, BBQ
                                barrels, 4 turrets, Vault in the
                                greenhouse in the middle.

    ICE RINK      3000 x 1700   Slippery floor with a few rubber-mat
                                patches where you get normal grip.
                                Bananas become deadly. 6 snowman
                                turrets.

    LAVA LAB      3000 x 1700   Lava rivers crossed by narrow bridges,
                                lab walls, chemical barrels everywhere,
                                8 security turrets. With knockback,
                                pushing people into the lava is very
                                satisfying.

    FACTORY       3200 x 1800   A network of conveyor belts that reverse
                                direction every 8 seconds. Crates and
                                barrels riding on the belts become
                                moving cover (and moving bombs). 6 turrets.

    PINBALL       2800 x 1600   Bumpers bounce players and bullets.
                                Bonus lanes are lined with crates. The
                                turrets are "targets" that drop extra
                                orbs.

    DARK HOUSE    2800 x 1600   Rooms and corridors. You only see a
                                circle around yourself. Muzzle flashes,
                                burning bushes and explosions light
                                things up. 4 turrets with flashlights.

    JUNGLE RUINS  3200 x 1800   Mostly bushes, with crumbling ruin
                                walls. The temple in the middle holds
                                the Vault, guarded by 2 turrets.
                                Bring explosives to clear the grass!


---------------------------------------------------------------------
11. GHOSTS (nobody sits out)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Eliminated players keep playing as ghosts until the round ends.
    - Ghosts float through walls, can't be hurt and get a wider camera
      view (1.5x).
    - Ghosts can't do direct damage, but every 6 seconds a ghost can:
        * SPOOK (shoot button): emit a "BOO!" shockwave that pushes
          nearby living players and XP orbs slightly away, OR
        * DROP BANANA (ability button): leave a phantom banana peel
          that lasts for 8 seconds.
    - PROP POSSESSION (New!): A ghost can float into any unbroken Crate
      or Explosive Barrel to "possess" it. While inside, the ghost can
      slowly scoot the object around (at 30% player speed) or make it
      wobble to freak out living players! Getting shot breaks the crate
      and pops the ghost back out.
    - Ghosts are see-through, so they never block the view.
    - In team mode, a ghost can float to a teammate to get revived
      (section 2).
    - Ghosts can never decide a round on their own. When only 2 living
      players remain (1v1), ghost cooldowns increase from 6s to 10s so
      the final duel stays fair.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
12. SCORING, CATCH-UP AND BALANCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  SCORING
    - Surviving is the only way to score: the last player (or team)
      alive wins the round and gets +1 point. KOs give XP, not points.
    - If the last players die at the same moment, the round is a draw
      and nobody scores.
    - First to 3 / 5 / 7 points wins the match.

  The danger with stacking upgrades and levels is a SNOWBALL: the
  winner gets stronger and keeps winning. The rules below push the
  other way, so each extra win is harder to get and players who are
  behind can come back.

  LEADER RULES ("being hunted")
    1. NO DRAFT: the round winner skips the next upgrade draft.
    2. CROWN & PULSING RADAR: the match leader wears a crown and pulses
       on everyone's minimap (and screen-edge arrows) for 1.5s every 4
       seconds.
    3. BOUNTY: knocking out the crown holder gives +40 XP, heals the
       attacker 30 HP, and drops a free Pickup Box. The bounty grows
       +20 XP for each round the leader has won in a row.
    4. SLOWER XP: the leader earns 0.7x XP (tuned up slightly from 0.6x
       so smashing crates still feels rewarding).
    5. MATCH POINT "BOSS MODE": When a player (or team) is 1 win away
       from winning the match, the round starts with a siren:
       "⚠️ ALEX IS ON MATCH POINT! STOP THEM! ⚠️". They grow 15% larger,
       glow with golden fire, and their bounty is doubled. It creates
       an unforgettable "everyone team up on the boss" moment.

  UNDERDOG RULES ("comeback")
    6. MORE CARDS & REROLL: the player(s) with the fewest wins get 4
       draft cards instead of 3, plus a 1-use "Reroll" button per draft.
    7. MORE XP: the player(s) with the fewest wins earn 1.5x XP.
    8. LEVEL CATCH-UP: at the start of each round, anyone more than 2
       levels below the highest-level player is raised to (highest
       level - 2). Nobody falls hopelessly behind.
    9. UNDERDOG SHIELD: the player(s) with the fewest wins start each
       round with a 25 HP shield (blue outline, absorbs damage first).
   10. THE GOLDEN SUPPLY DROP: Starting from Round 3, a Golden "?" Box
       parachutes into the map at 20 seconds (shown on the minimap). It
       contains an Overcharged Pickup (e.g., Dual Miniguns, Mega Giant
       Mode, or a Full Heal + Shield). Underdogs open it instantly on
       touch; the match leader takes 2.5 seconds of standing on it to
       unlock it.

  Everyone else earns 1.0x XP and gets 3 cards. In team mode all of
  these rules apply per team, using team scores.

  POWER CURVE (keep fights about the same length)
    Damage and HP both grow at a similar rate, so the number of hits
    needed to knock someone out stays at about 5-9 all match long.

                       Level   Max HP (approx.)   Bullet damage
      Round 1            1         100               20
      Mid match          5         150-200           25-30
      Final rounds      8-10       220-350           35-45

  All of these numbers live in one config file (config.js) so they can
  be tuned after playtests.

14. VISUAL AND AUDIO STYLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Visuals
    - Simple flat shapes drawn on an HTML canvas: round blob characters
      with expressive eyes (eyes squint when shooting, go wide when
      near an explosion, turn into X_X on KO), bright player colours
      and a dark floor so bullets stand out.
    - Bullets take the shooter's colour, so you can tell who is
      shooting at you. The final bullet in a magazine glows bright yellow
      and is slightly larger so everyone can see an empty-reload coming.
    - Emoji are used for hats, items and effects (🍌 🐑 🥔 👑).
    - There are no image files. Everything is drawn in code, so it
      stays light and loads quickly on phones.
    - With the camera, only what's on screen is drawn.

  Audio
    - Short sound effects (pew, boing, baa, fart, boom, level-up, rising
      pitch ticks for trickle-reloading) generated in the browser with
      the Web Audio API, so no audio files are needed.
    - Phones only allow sound after a tap; the first tap unlocks audio.
    - The mute button is in the corner. Sound is on by default on
      laptops (the "room speakers") and off on phones. The choice is
      remembered.

15. TECHNICAL DESIGN
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Platform
    - Static site on GitHub Pages, plain JavaScript modules, Canvas 2D,
      no build step.
    - Players connect directly to the host browser through PeerJS
      (WebRTC). PeerJS's free public server is only used to find each
      other.
    - Maximum of 8 players.

  VERSION NUMBER
    - The home screen title in index.html shows the version:
      <h1>Blast Party v0.4</h1>.
    - EVERY COMMIT increases it by one (v0.3 -> v0.4 -> v0.5 ...), so
      everyone can check they have the latest build after a deploy.
    - The title is the ONLY place the version is written. A small
      script in index.html reads it and loads every code file with the
      version attached (js/main.js?v0.4, ...), so after a deploy no
      browser keeps running old cached code.
    - VERSION CHECK: when joining, the guest sends its version and the
      host its own. If they differ, joining stops with a clear message
      ("Version mismatch: host has v0.4, you have v0.3. Reload the
      page.") instead of a broken or blank game screen.

  The host runs the game (host-authoritative)
    - The HOST device runs the only real simulation, at 60 steps per
      second: movement, bullets, auto-reloads, hits, abilities, XP,
      turrets and rounds.
    - Every device, including the host's own:
        1. sends its input to the host about 30 times per second.
           Button presses are sent as counters, so a press is never
           lost or counted twice.
        2. draws the world from the snapshots the host sends back,
           30 per second.
    - Devices draw other players slightly in the past and smoothly
      interpolate between snapshots (70 ms on good Wi-Fi, up to 200 ms
      on a bad connection), with brief extrapolation if packets drop.
    - YOUR OWN character is predicted: movement, dash, and ammo trickle/
      auto-reload UI update immediately on your screen and reconcile
      smoothly with the host.

  Bad connections
    - Two WebRTC channels per guest: reliable/ordered for lobby, draft,
      and results; fast/unordered for snapshots and inputs.
    - Ping indicator in the HUD (green <80 ms, yellow <200 ms, red >200 ms).

  Bigger maps and the camera
    - Area-of-interest culling: the host sends each player only what's
      near their camera view plus minimap data (zone, turrets, pulsing
      crown radar, golden crate, and shooting blips).

  Disconnects and reconnects
    - Up to 60s auto-reconnect with slot preservation and a 10s game
      pause ("⏸ Waiting for Bob to reconnect…").

17. DECISIONS AND OPEN QUESTIONS (UPDATED FOR v0.4)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  DECIDED (New in v0.4)
    - Manual reload vs Auto-reload: 100% AUTO-RELOAD. Removed the R key
      so laptop players don't have a mechanical advantage over phone
      players. Added trickle-reload (after 0.8s of not firing) and
      2-bullet dash reloads so combat feels fluid and never frustrating.
    - Turret & Vault XP: SPLIT IT. Half goes straight to whoever dealt
      the killing blow (so shooting objectives from afar is always worth
      it), and half drops as orbs to spark close-range fights.
    - Odd number of players in Team vs Team: YES, give the smaller team
      the "Underdog Squad" buff (+25% HP, +15% reload speed, 1.5s
      revives) instead of forcing someone to sit out or adding bots.
    - Crown holder on the minimap: PULSE EVERY 4 SECONDS (for 1.5s)
      instead of continuous tracking. It keeps the leader hunted while
      still letting them use bushes and corners to outplay attackers.
    - More than two teams (2v2v2): NO for now. Two colours (Red/Blue)
      are much easier to read in chaotic fights on a phone screen.

  OPEN (For v0.5 playtesting)
    - Does the 2-bullet Dash Reload make the DASH MASTER upgrade too
      strong when combined with PARTING GIFT (intentionally staying low
      on ammo to spam final-bullet explosions)?
    - Should ghosts be able to possess destroyed turret bases to fire
      harmless "paintball" shots that briefly splat an enemy's screen?
