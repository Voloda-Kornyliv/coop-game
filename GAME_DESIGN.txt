=====================================================================
  BLAST PARTY  (working title)
  Game design document, v0.1, 2026-10-13
=====================================================================

  A top-down, every-player-for-themselves arena shooter for 2-8
  friends in the same room. Everyone plays in the browser on their own
  phone or laptop. Rounds are short and chaotic, and there are lots of
  silly abilities. The aim is to make people laugh, shout and
  immediately want "one more round".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESIGN PILLARS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. FUNNY BEFORE FAIR
     Abilities should make the room laugh: slipping on a banana,
     getting turned into a sheep, swapping places at the worst moment.
     When a balance choice comes up, pick the funnier option, as long
     as it doesn't feel cheap.

  2. PICK UP AND PLAY IN 10 SECONDS
     Open the link, type your name, enter the code, play. No accounts,
     installs or tutorials. The controls explain themselves.

  3. PHONE IS A FIRST-CLASS CONTROLLER
     Every feature must work well with two thumbs on a phone. Laptops
     are supported, but nothing may need a mouse or keyboard.

  4. SHORT ROUNDS, NOBODY WAITS LONG
     Rounds last 60-90 seconds. Eliminated players keep playing as
     ghosts, so nobody just sits and watches.

  5. YOU CAN SEE EVERYTHING
     The whole arena is on screen at once, on every device. With no
     hidden information, friends can point at the screen and yell
     "behind you!"


---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE LOOP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  LOBBY  ->  ROUND  ->  RESULTS + ABILITY DRAFT  ->  ROUND  -> ... -> WINNER

  - The host creates a game. Friends join with the code (this already
    works).
  - The host picks the match length (first to 3 / 5 / 7 round wins)
    and presses START.
  - ROUND: everyone spawns at the edges of the arena. The last player
    alive wins the round. After 45 seconds the arena starts to shrink,
    so rounds can't drag on.
  - Between rounds, every player drafts a new upgrade (section 6).
    Upgrades stack for the rest of the match, so players get stronger
    and weirder every round.
  - The first player to reach the target number of round wins wins the
    match. An awards screen follows (section 10), then "Play again".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CAMERA AND ARENA
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Fixed top-down camera that shows the WHOLE arena, scaled to fit the
    screen. There is no scrolling and no split screen.
  - The arena is a 16:9 rectangle (logical size 1600 x 900 units), so
    it fills a landscape phone screen.
  - Phones play in LANDSCAPE. In portrait, show a "rotate your phone"
    screen.
  - The arena is sized for up to 8 players. Players are about 40 units
    across, which stays easy to see on a small phone screen.
  - Each player's own character has a bright outline and a small arrow
    above it, so you can find yourself instantly.

  Arena contents:
    - Walls: solid and indestructible. They block bullets and movement.
    - Crates: break after 3 hits and sometimes drop a pickup.
    - Hazards: change per map (section 8).
    - Shrinking zone: a ring that closes in after 45 seconds. Standing
      outside it deals damage over time.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONTROLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  PHONE (landscape, touch)
    Left thumb:   virtual joystick to MOVE (appears wherever you first
                  touch on the left half of the screen).
    Right thumb:  virtual joystick to AIM. Holding it pushed out fires
                  automatically (twin-stick style).
    Buttons beside the right stick:
      [DASH]      short burst of speed with a brief moment of
                  invulnerability.
      [ABILITY]   your drafted ability. Tap to cast it in the aim
                  direction, or drag from the button to aim it
                  precisely.
      [PICKUP]    the single-use item from the map (greyed out when
                  empty).
    Extras: the phone vibrates when you get hit (Android only; iOS
    browsers don't support it). The game requests fullscreen and keeps
    the screen awake while you play.

  LAPTOP (keyboard + mouse)
    WASD / arrow keys   move
    Mouse               aim
    Left click (hold)   shoot
    Space               dash
    Q or right click    ability
    E                   pickup item

  GAMEPAD (later): twin sticks, triggers to shoot, face buttons for
  abilities, using the browser's Gamepad API.

  FAIRNESS BETWEEN PHONE AND MOUSE
    A mouse aims much more precisely than a thumb. To balance this:
    - Bullets are large, fairly slow and easy to see, so dodging
      matters more than pinpoint aim.
    - Touch aiming gets light aim assist: shots bend slightly toward the
      nearest enemy inside a narrow cone.
    - Most abilities are area-based rather than precision-based.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PLAYER BASICS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Health:       100 HP. The basic bullet deals 20 (5 hits to eliminate).
  Movement:     fairly fast and responsive, with no inertia (except on
                ice).
  Basic gun:    fires about 4 shots per second with unlimited ammo.
  Dash:         about 200 units over 0.15 seconds, with a 2 second
                cooldown and invulnerability while dashing.
  Knockback:    every hit pushes the target back a little. This adds
                comedy and lets you push people into hazards.
  Elimination:  the player bursts into confetti, a big "KO!" appears,
                and the kill feed shows a line like
                "Alex BANANA'D Bob".
  After death:  you become a GHOST (section 7).


---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ABILITIES
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Each player has three kinds of power:

    A) ONE ACTIVE ABILITY (the [ABILITY] button)
       Picked in the first draft. It has a cooldown and can be swapped
       for a different one in later drafts.

    B) PASSIVE UPGRADES (stack all match long)
       Drafted between rounds. Each one permanently changes your
       character or gun.

    C) MAP PICKUPS (the [PICKUP] button)
       Single-use items that spawn in the arena. You can carry one at a
       time.

  DRAFT RULES
    - After each round, every player is shown 3 random cards and picks
      1. Everyone picks at the same time, with a 15 second timer that
      picks randomly when it runs out.
    - Catch-up rule: players with the FEWEST round wins are offered 4
      cards instead of 3. Whoever leads the match gets only 2.
    - The first draft (before round 1) always offers ACTIVE abilities.
      After that, cards are mostly passives, with a chance to offer a
      new active ability.

  --- 6A. ACTIVE ABILITIES (cooldown 6-15 s) ---------------------------

  Movement
    BLINK         Short teleport in the aim direction.
    SWAP          Fire a slow orb. Whoever it hits swaps places with you.
    GRAPPLE       A hook that pulls you to a wall, or pulls a player to
                  you.
    BOUNCE PAD    Drop a pad. Anyone who steps on it (you included) is
                  launched across the arena.

  Damage
    SHOTGUN       A wide burst of 7 pellets with big knockback.
    BOOMERANG     Flies out and comes back, hitting on both passes.
    HOMING ROCKET A slow rocket that follows the nearest enemy and can be
                  shot down.
    CHAIN ZAP     Lightning that jumps between up to 4 nearby players.
    SNOWBALL      Grows bigger and stronger the longer it rolls.
    BLACK HOLE    Pulls every player and bullet toward one point for
                  2 seconds.

  Defense
    BUBBLE        Blocks all damage for 1.5 seconds.
    MIRROR        Reflects bullets back at the shooter for 1 second.
    DECOY         Creates a fake copy of you that walks forward. Hitting
                  it causes a small explosion.
    VANISH        Invisible for 3 seconds. Footprints still give you
                  away.

  Chaos (the funny ones)
    BANANA        Throw a banana peel. Anyone who steps on it slides
                  helplessly, spinning around.
    SHEEPIFY      Turns the target into a sheep for 2 seconds: no
                  shooting, they can only "baa" (the shoot button plays
                  a sound).
    BIG HEAD      Gives every enemy a huge head (a bigger hitbox) for
                  4 seconds.
    SHRINK RAY    The target becomes tiny, fast and weak for 4 seconds.
    REVERSE       The target's movement controls are reversed for
                  3 seconds.
    HOT POTATO    Sticks a bomb to the target. Touching another player
                  passes it on. It explodes after 5 seconds.
    DISCO BALL    Everyone nearby is forced to dance (spin in place) for
                  1.5 seconds.
    FART CLOUD    A green cloud that does damage over time and pushes
                  people away. Plays a sound, of course.

  --- 6B. PASSIVE UPGRADES (stack all match) ---------------------------

    BOUNCY BULLETS     Bullets bounce off walls once (stacks: more
                       bounces).
    SPLIT SHOT         +1 bullet per shot, fired in a spread.
    BIG BULLETS        Bullets are 50% larger and deal +5 damage.
    FAST FEET          +15% movement speed.
    QUICK TRIGGER      +20% fire rate.
    VAMPIRE            Heal 5 HP every time you hit someone.
    THICK SKIN         +30 maximum HP (your character looks chunkier).
    DASH MASTER        Dash cooldown cut in half. Dashing leaves a fire
                       trail.
    LAST LAUGH         Explode when you die, damaging anyone nearby.
    PIERCING           Bullets pass through the first player they hit.
    MAGNET             Pickups fly toward you. Bullets bend slightly
                       toward enemies.
    COOLDOWN CLOCK     Ability cooldown -25%.
    TINY               You are 25% smaller and harder to hit, with
                       -10 maximum HP.
    RICOCHET RAGE      Each bullet that hits a wall speeds up and deals
                       +5 damage.

  --- 6C. MAP PICKUPS (single use, spawn every ~10 s) ------------------

    MEDKIT        Restore 40 HP.
    MINIGUN       5 seconds of very fast fire.
    LANDMINE      Place an invisible mine.
    SPEED BOOTS   Double speed for 4 seconds.
    GIANT MODE    Grow huge for 4 seconds: push others around and take
                  half damage.
    CONFUSION     Every OTHER player's screen is flipped upside down for
                  3 seconds. (This is where the shouting comes from.)
    TELEPORT ALL  Every player is moved to a random spot.
    RANDOM        Gives a random active ability as a one-shot.

  Pickups appear in glowing boxes. You can't see what's inside until
  you grab it.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. GHOSTS (nobody sits out)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Eliminated players keep playing as ghosts until the round ends.
    - Ghosts float through walls and can't be hurt.
    - Ghosts can't do damage, but they can be annoying:
        * Every 8 seconds a ghost can drop a banana peel or spook a
          living player (a short push).
        * Ghosts can pick up and throw crates.
    - Ghosts are see-through, so they never block the view.
    - Ghosts can never decide a round on their own. They annoy, and the
      living still have to finish each other off.

  This gives plenty of revenge comedy and keeps eliminated friends
  watching the screen.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MAPS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Each round uses a random map. The host can lock one map in the lobby.

    BACKYARD    The basic map: a few walls and lots of crates. Good for
                learning.
    ICE RINK    Slippery floor (movement has inertia). Bananas become
                deadly.
    LAVA LAB    Lava pools that burn. With knockback, pushing people
                in is very satisfying.
    CONVEYOR    Conveyor belts drag everyone sideways. Some belts
                switch direction.
    PINBALL     Bumpers bounce players and bullets around.
    DARK HOUSE  The light shrinks to a circle around each player.
                Muzzle flashes reveal positions. (Breaks pillar 5 on
                purpose, as a special map.)


---------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SCORING AND CATCH-UP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The last player alive wins the round (+1 point). If the last
    players die at the same moment, the round is a draw and no point is
    awarded.
  - First to 3 / 5 / 7 points wins the match (the host chooses).
  - CROWN: the current match leader wears a crown. Eliminating the
    crown holder heals you 30 HP. This puts a target on the leader's
    back, and that's the point.
  - Players who are behind get more draft cards (section 6).
  - REVENGE: whoever eliminated you is marked on your screen next
    round. Killing them shows a big "REVENGE!" banner.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
10. PARTY FEATURES (playing in the same room)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Big, readable announcements that friends see on each other's
    screens: "KO!", "DOUBLE KO!", "REVENGE!", "LAST ONE STANDING!".
  - A funny kill feed with a verb for each ability:
    "zapped", "bananaed", "sheeped", "yeeted into lava", "potatoed".
  - Emote wheel in the lobby, between rounds and as a ghost: a few
    big emoji bubbles above your character.
  - Every player chooses a colour and a hat in the lobby (crown,
    propeller cap, chef hat, traffic cone...).
  - End-of-match awards, so everyone gets a moment:
      Champion            the most round wins
      Banana King         the most enemies slipped
      Kamikaze            the most self-eliminations
      Ghost of the Match  the most ghost annoyances
      Pacifist            the least damage dealt
      Unlucky             took the most damage
  - Strong game feel: screen shake, hit flashes, knockback, short
    slow motion on the final kill of a round.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
11. VISUAL AND AUDIO STYLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Visuals
    - Simple flat shapes drawn on an HTML canvas: round blob characters
      with eyes, bright saturated player colours and a dark,
      low-contrast floor so bullets stand out.
    - Bullets take the shooter's colour, so you can tell who is
      shooting at you.
    - No image files are needed for the MVP. Everything is drawn in
      code, so it stays light and loads quickly on phones.

  Audio
    - Short synthesized sound effects (pew, boing, baa, fart, splat)
      generated in the browser with the Web Audio API, so no audio
      files are needed.
    - Phones only allow sound after a tap. The "Join" or "Start" button
      unlocks audio.
    - Mute button in the corner. Sound is on by default on laptops,
      which become the "room speakers".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
12. TECHNICAL DESIGN
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Platform
    - Static site on GitHub Pages with plain JavaScript, Canvas 2D and
      no build step.
    - Builds on the existing lobby, which uses PeerJS / WebRTC
      peer-to-peer connections.
    - Maximum of 8 players, matching the lobby.

  The host runs the game (host-authoritative)
    - The HOST device runs the real simulation: movement, bullets,
      hits, abilities and rounds.
    - Each player device only:
        1. sends its input to the host about 30 times per second
           (move direction, aim direction, buttons pressed, sequence
           number), and
        2. draws the world state the host sends back.
    - The host sends world snapshots about 20 times per second
      (positions, HP, bullets, effects).
    - Player devices draw the world about 100 ms in the past and
      smoothly interpolate between two snapshots, so movement looks
      smooth even with an uneven connection.
    - Your own character is predicted locally (it moves the moment you
      press) and corrected to match the host. This can be added after
      the MVP if the game feels sluggish.
    - Because everyone is in the same room on the same Wi-Fi, latency
      should be very low. That's an advantage of this game idea.

  Recommendation: the host should be a LAPTOP when possible. Running
  the full simulation for 8 players is easy for a laptop, but it uses
  more battery and processing on a phone. Phones can still host smaller
  games.

  Data sizes
    - A snapshot is roughly 8 players x ~30 bytes + bullets + effects,
      under 2 KB as compact arrays. This is no problem on local Wi-Fi.
    - Use short keys / arrays instead of verbose JSON objects.

  Disconnects
    - A player who disconnects mid-round is eliminated, and their score
      is kept in case they rejoin with the same name.
    - If the host leaves, the match ends (same as the current lobby).
      Host migration is out of scope.

  Proposed file structure
    index.html     screens: home, lobby, game, draft, results
    style.css
    net.js         PeerJS connection layer (extracted from app.js)
    lobby.js       lobby UI (the current app.js logic)
    game/
      sim.js       host-only simulation: physics, combat, rounds
      abilities.js data table + behaviour for every ability
      maps.js      arena layouts
      render.js    canvas drawing, effects, interpolation
      input.js     touch joysticks, keyboard/mouse, gamepad
      audio.js     Web Audio sound effects
      ui.js        HUD, draft cards, announcements, awards

  Design abilities as DATA: each ability is one entry in a table
  (name, cooldown, icon, a cast function, a kill-feed verb). This makes
  adding new abilities quick and cheap.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
13. BUILD PLAN (MILESTONES)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  M1  "IT MOVES"
      Host START button. Arena with walls. Players move with
      touch / keyboard. Positions sync to every device.
      Done when two phones and a laptop can see each other running
      around smoothly.

  M2  "IT SHOOTS"
      Basic gun, HP, knockback, dash, elimination, last-one-standing
      round win, scoreboard, next round.
      Done when you can play a real match, first to 5.

  M3  "IT'S FUNNY"
      The ability draft with the first 8 actives (BANANA, SHEEPIFY,
      BLINK, SHOTGUN, BOOMERANG, BUBBLE, HOT POTATO, BLACK HOLE) and
      6 passives. Kill feed and announcements. Sound effects.

  M4  "NOBODY WAITS"
      Ghosts, map pickups, the shrinking zone, the crown and catch-up
      rules.

  M5  "MORE MORE MORE"
      The rest of the abilities and maps, hats/colours, emotes, awards,
      gamepad support.

  M6  POLISH
      Screen shake and slow motion, balance pass, performance tuning on
      older phones, local prediction if needed.
