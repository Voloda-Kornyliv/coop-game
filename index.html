    How to play

    Last one standing wins the round. Between rounds everyone picks an upgrade, and they stack. Dead players come back as ghosts who can annoy the living.

    Phone (landscape): left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. Buttons: ability, dash, pickup.

    Laptop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Space dash, Q / right-click ability, E pickup, 1–4 emotes.

    Tip: a laptop makes the best host.
